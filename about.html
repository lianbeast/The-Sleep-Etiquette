About us

Sleepwear
with standards.

We started The Sleep Etiquette because sleepwear had split into two camps: pajamas you would never be seen in, and fashion you could never sleep in.

Shop the collection

The gap

Between pajamas
and fashion.

01The old pajama

Boxy, baggy, resigned. Made to be hidden, not worn. Comfort treated as an excuse to stop designing.

02The fashion piece

Beautiful, and completely impractical after 10 p.m. Scratchy trims, dry-clean tags, fits that only photograph.

03Our answer

Sleepwear that takes itself as seriously as your day wardrobe — cut, styled, and finished like the clothes you choose.

Fit philosophy

One pattern,
three heights.

The same silhouette, graded for your frame. Not “shorter.” Reconsidered.

01Petite

Sleeves, rise, and hem shortened proportionally — never cropped, never pooling.

02Regular

Our base pattern. A relaxed line designed to look intentional at breakfast.

03Tall

Extra length through the body and leg, with a rise that stays where it should.
