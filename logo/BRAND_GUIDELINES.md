# The Sleep Etiquette — Brand Identity Guidelines

## Overview

**Name:** The Sleep Etiquette  
**Tagline:** Sleep well. Dress better.  
**Founded:** 2026  
**Positioning:** Fashion-first sleepwear, XS–4X, six colourways  
**Brand adjectives:** restrained · warm · considered · editorial

---

## The Mark

### Primary symbol

A serif **E/T ligature** inside a disc frame. The shared vertical spine reads as both the "E" of Etiquette and the "T" of The; the full-width top arm confirms the T at a glance. Slab serifs at every arm end and the spine foot give the mark weight and authority.

The disc frame references a clothing label's woven tag without being literal — it signals "this is a garment brand" through suggestion, not illustration.

### Lockups

| Variant | Use case |
|---------|----------|
| **Horizontal** (symbol left, wordmark right) | Header, footer, email signatures |
| **Stacked** (symbol above wordmark) | Social avatar, favicons, narrow spaces |
| **Symbol only** | App icon, embroidery, small surfaces |

### Minimum size

- **Horizontal:** 180 px wide (symbol 48 × 48)
- **Symbol only:** 32 × 32 px
- **Small-size cut:** for favicons and app icons at 16–32 px where the full E/T arms collapse into illegibility

---

## Clear Space

Maintain a clear-space zone equal to **one disc radius** (≈ 50% of symbol height) around the mark on all sides. No other elements may enter this zone.

## Colour Palette

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|------|------|-----|-----|------|-----|
| **Ink** | After-dark | #222222 | 34, 34, 34 | 90, 85, 80, 85 | Primary text, mark on light |
| **Brand accent** | Cocoa | #6B5747 | 107, 87, 71 | 50, 55, 55, 20 | Wordmark, mono variant, buttons |
| **Ground** | Milk | #F7F3EF | 247, 243, 239 | 0, 2, 3, 0 | Background, reversed mark ground |
| **White** | Paper | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | Reversed mark, card backgrounds |

**Colour rules:**
- Use ink (#222222) for the mark on milk or paper backgrounds.
- Use cocoa (#6B5747) for the one-colour/matcha variant — it owns the palette because no other sleepwear brand uses a brown accent.
- The reversed white mark goes on after-dark, cocoa, or any colourway background.

## Typography

**Primary typeface:** Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts)  
**Role:** Headlines, the wordmark, editorial bodies  
**Weight:** 600 for "THE SLEEP", 400 italic for "ETIQUETTE"

**Secondary typeface:** Instrument Sans (Google Fonts)  
**Role:** Navigation, body copy, labels, UI  
**Weight:** 400 regular, 500 medium for emphasis

**Letter-spacing:**
- Wordmark: `letter-spacing: 0.10em` (THE SLEEP), `letter-spacing: 0.12em` (ETIQUETTE italic)
- Eyebrow text: `letter-spacing: 0.08em`, uppercase, 500 weight

## Approved Usage

### On colours

The mark reads correctly on:
- Milk (#F7F3EF) — primary background
- Paper (#FFFFFF) — card/section backgrounds
- After-dark (#222222) — reversed mark
- Cocoa (#6B5747) — one-colour variant
- Matcha (#B8C2A8) — **use the white mark only** (contrast fails with ink on matcha)

### On product imagery

When placed over product photos, use the white mark or a solid-colour opacity block behind it. Never place the ink mark directly over high-contrast imagery.

### Embroidery

Use the symbol-only variant in cocoa thread on a milk garment, or ink thread on a cocoa garment. The small-size cut is preferred for pocket-scale embroidery.

---

## Do Not

- Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark
- Add shadows, gradients, or outlines
- Change the colour relationships (ink → white, cocoa → ink, etc.)
- Place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Use the full E/T ligature below 16 px without the small-size cut
- Combine with competing marks or logos
- Alter the spacing between the symbol and wordmark
