# Brag Plan: The Sleep Etiquette

## Planning rubric

1. **What is the app?** A fashion-first sleepwear site built around a modular wardrobe: breathable separates, six tonal shades, inclusive sizing, and a set builder.
2. **Strongest claim:** "Sleep well. Dress better."
3. **Visual hook:** The warm bedroom hero followed by the six editorial portrait colorways.
4. **Actual UI to show:** Hero, six-color wardrobe, and the set-builder interaction where top, bottom, and shade resolve into one selected set.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20.2 seconds. Four scenes leave enough time for premium image holds and readable copy.
6. **Tone:** Polished. Creative direction: quiet premium fashion film with magazine pacing.
7. **Audio:** Steady, clean music at restrained volume, two soft reveal hits, and one quiet selection click. Background warmth responds subtly to music energy.
8. **Share caption:** Made The Sleep Etiquette - a fashion-first sleepwear site built around six quiet shades, mix-and-match sets, and one rule: sleep well, dress better.
9. **User flow:** Enter through the collection story, choose a top and bottom, choose a shade, then see the set summary.

## What is this app?

The Sleep Etiquette presents women's sleepwear with daywear-level consideration: breathable fabrics, six mix-and-match shades, XS-4X sizing, and Petite, Regular, and Tall lengths.

## The angle

Treat the site like a compact fashion campaign, not a software demo. The film moves from the bedroom atmosphere into the six-color wardrobe, then briefly demonstrates the set builder before closing on the brand's material and fit standard. Every line and product image comes from the site.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Open inside the real bedroom image. A slow camera push finds the serif lockup: "Sleep well. Dress better." The motion is calm but immediate, with the room and typography already legible by 1.0s.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Six real model portraits assemble into one editorial color story while Matcha, Butter, Milk, Cocoa, Sky, and Petal labels settle below.
- The set-builder scene simulates choosing a top, bottom, and Milk shade, then resolves the real summary: "Short sleeve shirt + Long pant" and "10% set pricing."
- The final proof scene pairs product detail imagery with "Made for sleep. Cut like clothing." and the real size range.

## Outro / punchline

The Sleep Etiquette wordmark holds beside "Made for sleep. Cut like clothing." and the real fit range. The last musical accent lands before a clean final hold.

## User flow worth showing

Collection entry -> choose top and bottom -> choose shade -> selected set summary.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium fashion film with magazine pacing
- Interpretation: Four long, composed scenes; serif-led copy; restrained parallax; soft crossfades; no aggressive zooms, novelty effects, or dense feature lists.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.2 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: Milk `#F7F3EF`
- Accent: Matcha `#B8C2A8`, with Butter `#E8D89A` reserved for small highlights
- Text: After Dark `#222222`
- Supporting palette: Cocoa `#6B5747`, Sky `#7D8FA1`, Petal `#D7BFC0`, white `#FFFFFF`
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond, with Georgia fallback only if the local font asset cannot be prepared
- Body font: Instrument Sans, with system sans fallback only if the local font asset cannot be prepared
- Strongest visual element: six real editorial model portraits aligned with the collection palette

## Share copy (draft)

Made The Sleep Etiquette - a fashion-first sleepwear site built around six quiet shades, mix-and-match sets, and one rule: sleep well, dress better.

## Audio direction

- Role: restrained premium bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: start at 0s, volume 0.24, short fade-in, subtle fade under the final 1.2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at 109.96 BPM. Major landings target 8.19s, 13.11s, and 17.47s. Portrait arrivals use 5.34s, 6.00s, 6.56s, 7.09s, 7.64s, and 8.19s, then hold the complete set.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music bass gently changes the hero light and image-panel presence. No waveform, equalizer, particle field, or text pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse; one warm soft impact at the colorway reveal, one quiet click on the set choice, one warm accent on the closing proof
- Audio-coupled moments: six portrait sequence, simulated set selection, final wordmark landing
- Restraint rule: sound never competes with copy or makes the fashion film feel like an app commercial

### Scene 1 - Considered sleepwear - 4.39s

The real bedroom hero fills the frame. A Milk-toned light pool separates the copy from the image. "Sleepwear, considered" appears first; "Sleep well. Dress better." follows at campaign scale. Small edition copy anchors the lower-right edge.

Sequential/interaction: none

Audio intent: music arrives softly; no opening impact

Audio-coupled idea: background light and image presence breathe subtly with the bed

Music: vol. 12, low and steady

Transition mood: soft editorial crossfade -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - Six shades - 6.54s

Six real colorway portraits assemble from one featured crop into an aligned six-panel collection. "Six shades. One point of view." stays readable while the labels Matcha, Butter, Milk, Cocoa, Sky, and Petal settle beneath the images.

Sequential/interaction: yes - six portraits arrive on the beat grid at 5.34s through 8.19s, then the full collection holds for more than 2.5s

Audio intent: one warm reveal impact on the final portrait; no sound on every item

Audio-coupled idea: portrait sequence aligned to every beat while labels remain held long enough to read

Music: vol. 12

Transition mood: clean lateral slide -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - Build your set - 4.91s

A real Milk model portrait and product image sit beside a simplified recreation of the set builder. Short sleeve shirt and Long pant lock in, the Milk shade receives a selection ring, and the summary resolves to "Short sleeve shirt + Long pant" with "10% set pricing."

Sequential/interaction: yes - simulate two choices and one shade selection, then reveal the selected-set summary

Audio intent: a quiet click supports the shade choice; music stays below the interaction

Audio-coupled idea: selection lands after the scene opens at 10.93s; summary settles near the strong cue at 13.11s

Music: vol. 12

Transition mood: gentle color dip into After Dark -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Cut like clothing - 4.36s

After Dark becomes the ground. Product detail imagery and a single Butter rule frame "Made for sleep. Cut like clothing." Supporting proof appears once: "XS-4X. Petite, Regular, Tall." A small wordmark anchors the final hold; no extra closing sentence competes with the proof.

Sequential/interaction: none

Audio intent: one warm accent at 17.47s, then music fades cleanly

Audio-coupled idea: fit proof and wordmark lock to the 17.47s strong cue

Music: vol. 12, fading in the final 1.2s
